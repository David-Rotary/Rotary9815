Rotary Red Energy Community Grants

Your club's project could be next

Red Energy pays $5 a month to District 9815 for every gas or electricity service on the Red Rotary D9815 Saver plan. That money comes back to clubs as grants for local community projects.

Grant sizes

Three grant categories

The Committee sets the amount available each year based on the fund balance, and may award fewer or smaller grants depending on the quality of applications.

Small Community Grant
Up to $1,000

Equipment, materials or a focused local initiative.

Standard Community Grant
$1,001 – $3,000

A well-scoped project with clear community benefit.

Major Community Grant
$3,001 – $5,000

Larger projects, ideally delivered by several clubs together.

How to apply

Six steps from idea to impact

  1. Talk to us earlyCheck your idea with the Committee Chair or your Rotary Community Leader. Projects involving several clubs are especially welcome.
  2. Get the application formA single two-page form: club details, a 100-word description, beneficiaries, dates and budget.
  3. Complete and signInclude your budget, any other funding sources and the applicant declaration.
  4. Email it to the CommitteeSend the completed form to the Committee Chair before the round closes.
  5. AssessmentThe Committee reviews all applications in the round against the seven criteria.
  6. Deliver and report backComplete within 12 months, send an acquittal with photos, and acknowledge the program.
